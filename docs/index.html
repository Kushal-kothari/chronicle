Features

Not just how long — on what, and why.

⏱

Automatic screen time tracking

Browse normally. Chronicle tracks time on every site in the background — switches with your tabs, pauses when you go idle.

🚫

Website blocker & daily limits

Set a daily limit per site. Hit it and the page is blocked — with a five-minute defer when you genuinely need it.

🍅

Focus timer & Pomodoro

Start a 25/45/60/90-minute focus session, name the work, rate it after. Build a log of your real deep work.

🗂

Smart categories

Sites auto-sort into Work, Learning, Social, Entertainment and News. Override once; it sticks.

🎯

Daily goals

"Under 2h on Social." "At least 1h Learning." A live ring tracks progress and nudges you before you cross the line.

📊

Weekly insights & CSV export

A plain-language weekly digest, a full dashboard with charts, and one-click CSV export of any date range.

Privacy

Your browsing data never leaves your device.

  • All data stored locally with Chrome's storage API — on your device only
  • No analytics SDKs, no ad trackers, no telemetry of any kind
  • No account, no login, no server
  • Uninstalling deletes everything
  • Fully open source — read every line

FAQ

Questions

Is Chronicle really free?

Yes — completely free and open source under the MIT license. No paid tier, no ads.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser, using Chrome's local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and there's no account to create.

Does it work as a website blocker?

Yes. Set a daily time limit per site; once reached, Chronicle blocks the page. You can also block sites outright.

Is there a Pomodoro / focus timer?

Yes — start focus sessions of 25/45/60/90 minutes (or open-ended) and run classic Pomodoro work/break cycles.

Can I export my data?

Yes — export any date range to CSV from the dashboard, and back up or restore all data.
